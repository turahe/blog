# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: hiring managers and tech recruiters evaluating Nur Wachid for roles. Secondary: potential clients and freelance leads. Context is a quick professional scan — portfolio, writing, and GitHub — before deciding whether to reach out.

## Product Purpose

Get contacted, interviewed, and hired. Success is a recruiter who opens work samples, trusts the craft, and initiates contact — not vanity traffic.

## Positioning

Senior engineer who ships real systems — not demos. Strong product taste is supporting proof, not the headline claim.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary and secondary CTA: primary is view projects / case studies; secondary is read the blog
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: Nur Wachid ships real systems — not demos.
- Belief ladder: (1) this person is a serious engineer, not a template portfolio; (2) they have shipped real work worth inspecting; (3) projects are the fastest way to judge fit
- Proof on hand: blog posts, GitHub profile/repos, and a resume PDF (add the PDF under `.impeccable/assets/proof/` when ready). Project entries already live in `src/data/projectsData.ts`.

## Brand Personality

Precise · capable · understated. The interface should leave a recruiter with **confidence** — sharp, capable, low drama. Voice is clear and technical without hype. Visual references lean agency/portfolio: bold typography, restrained color.

## Anti-references

Generic Tailwind starter blogs and the purple-gradient “AI SaaS” look. No template-fingerprint chrome, no stock gradient hero energy.

## Design Principles

- **Proof before pitch** — lead with shipped work; claims trail evidence.
- **Confidence without noise** — calm authority beats loud self-promotion.
- **Restraint is the taste signal** — bold type and sparse color earn trust; decoration burns it.
- **Serious engineer, not template** — every surface should fail the “starter blog” test.
- **Fast path to judgment** — make projects the shortest route from land to “I want to talk.”

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`. Maintain focus visibility and contrast for body and UI text.
